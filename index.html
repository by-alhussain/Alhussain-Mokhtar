Hi, I'm Alhussain

I'm a character designer and animation student who loves inventing characters and finding out who they are.

I like thinking about how a character moves, what they care about, and what small story lives behind their eyes. Animation is the reason I draw. Visual storytelling, where a shape, a pose or a glance can say something without a single word, is what I keep learning and keep falling for.
